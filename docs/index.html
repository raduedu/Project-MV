Project MV - lap replays

A racing driver made of software, learned by trial and error (reinforcement learning) in a simulator. Every lap below was driven by a trained driver and recorded 25 times per second. The grey ghost is the physics-perfect lap for the same car and circuit: the gap between them is how far from the limit the driver is. Circuits are generated by the project; cars are built from public data.
